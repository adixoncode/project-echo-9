# Phase 4A — image-first breakdown before geometry

Inputs viewed: actual `target-red-corridor.jpg` and actual iteration3 `after-clean.png`, not code-derived expectations. Baseline main8726d38. This is the already approved set-design phase; it is not owner acceptance of any future render.

## Large shapes and silhouette

The target is organized by thick octagonal thresholds: upright jambs, long diagonal shoulders, a broad overhead lintel and lower inclined service ledges. Nested frames have visible depth and feel like a load-bearing pressure hull. The near right mass is dark but has an articulated inward-facing edge; its purpose is to frame the lit passage. In iteration3 a broad black right wall consumes much of the image, while narrow bright edges and the ceiling ladder dominate. The octagonal whole is weaker than its individual strips.

## Middle forms and function

The target's wall equipment sits behind the structural face. Hatches, conduits, cable loops and lower pipe channels have different depths, with an intelligible hierarchy from hull to frame to service layer. Pipes are grouped into secondary channels. Iteration3 has repeated handles/panels, long prominent round tubes and closely spaced floor/ceiling bars. Their equal rhythm suggests ornament more than distinct bays with different maintenance functions. One open equipment cavity, a displaced cover and a connected valve/junction assembly can give a bay a reason to differ without filling it with random details.

## Negative space, human scale and attention

The target keeps the central walking strip clear and readable. Heavy foreground architecture, layered middle frames and the softer far opening form three planes. Perspective lines carry the eye into the red distance, rather than stopping at a crosspiece. Iteration3's broad floor bands, large polished tubes and exposed far transverse structure flatten this hierarchy. The fixed hero camera is oblique and will remain unchanged; the set must make the near structural edge meaningful within that view. Any alternate framing would be a separately labeled proposal, never a replacement of the canonical view.

## Thumbnail checklist — review at320px before surface polish

1. A thick octagonal threshold reads as one architectural silhouette, with visible diagonal shoulders and real axial depth.
2. Near frame, recessed middle bay and distant open passage are distinguishable without stronger fog or brighter exposure.
3. The right foreground has a purposeful layered edge/void; no giant featureless slab dominates half the frame.
4. The eye follows a clear walking strip and coherent pipe/service channels; floor/ceiling bars do not become the primary pattern.
5. At least one functional asymmetric maintenance landmark is readable, while large/medium structure remains stronger than small detail.

Production comparison retains iteration3 lights, material shaders, HDR/AgX and atmosphere. A separately identified neutral geometry render must reveal actual thickness, voids and occlusion. Shader polish is not the geometry gate. Final geometry/composition assessment belongs to the independent image-only critic and Adrian; technical tests cannot award it.
