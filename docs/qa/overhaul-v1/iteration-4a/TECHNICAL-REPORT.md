- Near bays have recessed doors and mechanical channels behind the structural plane. A negative-X maintenance pocket aroundz30–34 sets its rear hull atx−5.55, with a connected pump/filter/valve assembly and a displaced cover retained inside the recess. The near-right visual mass is interrupted by actual depth and equipment rather than screen-space paint. Other bay equipment has unequal widths and functional connections instead of identical handles everywhere.
- The near central walking strip has broad plates, with grating retained in side strips. Dense near ceiling tray/grille crossbars are suppressed. Oversized near upper risers are removed; supply runs sit behind the shoulders. This changes the hierarchy of large/middle forms without simply multiplying decorative geometry.
- Cheaper repeated construction remains farther along. The original flared exit hull and genuine through-passage are preserved. The existing hangar remains visible beyond it in the neutral diagnostic; its inherited transverse forms and lighting are not redesigned or hidden by a fake terminus or stronger fog.

All geometry exists continuously from every camera. There is no camera-threshold mesh substitution and no change of saved hero. The canonical oblique framing is retained for every main comparison; remaining framing limitations are visible evidence, not silently corrected by replacing the camera.

Final HTML raw/gzip sizes and identity are in [artifact-manifest.json](artifact-manifest.json); actual browser-reported allocation estimates and timings are in [capture](after-capture.json) and [route report](route/browser-report.json). GPU estimates cover persistent buffers/textures, not measured peak VRAM; small uniforms, temporary diagnostic readbacks and browser/driver overhead are excluded. More convincing geometry does not itself prove faster rendering on hardware.
