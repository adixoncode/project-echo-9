# Corridor HDR implementation

The corridor stores lighting and reflections in linear `rgba16float`, composes transmittance and in-scattering in another linear HDR target, extracts bright radiance for bloom, and applies exposure 1.35, filmic tone mapping and display gamma once in the final corridor display pass. The material and scattering passes do not tone-map. The construction archives show HDR-only and relighting before post effects.

All geometry uses the same depth attachment. MRT preserves both the original legacy display expression and pre-tone radiance, while corridor material output marks spatial coverage. Ray/room intersection clipped to the receiver depth covers air between the camera and a surface. The final shader copies the legacy display exactly where no corridor material/air contributes. This uses physical depth/coverage, not a camera-position threshold or alternate scene. HDR particle blending preserves destination coverage even when adding radiance.

`src/corridor-lights.js` holds seven authored sources. Fixture geometry/emission, direct GGX, two cached geometric shadow cameras, six real HDR room-probe captures and scattering consume the same positions, spectra and source powers. Bilinear 2×2 PCF compares each texel against analytically projected receiver-plane depth using the unperturbed triangle face normal. This removes false curved self-shadow bands on sloping panels without globally increasing bias or disabling real occlusion.

The main cool source moves to the camera side of the middle rib instead of illuminating away from that surface. Red pendant diffusers remain visible from the approved camera and the main red cone reaches the floor in front of its source. The final cool-key power is lower than the relighting checkpoint; exposure remains unchanged.

The local probe retains 128²×6 faces, eight mip levels, 128-sample GGX convolution and room-box parallax correction. Directional low-amplitude normal variation and roughness variation broaden metal responses. It is one cached local probe, not traced multi-bounce GI or screen-space reflection; it cannot reproduce every local viewpoint or occlusion.

Atmosphere integrates 24 fixed samples at half resolution. Same-table light radiance and the two geometric shadow maps feed a phase-function approximation. Eye-path transmittance uses a bounded corridor medium, denser in the far bays, with finite near-source quadrature footprint. Receiver-distance bilateral reconstruction limits geometry-edge bleeding; full-resolution transmittance uses the shared scene depth, including all four MSAA samples. This is approximate single scattering: it does not integrate source-to-sample extinction, multiple scattering or full volumetric transport. Static stills cannot establish temporal smoothness.

Bloom uses four tent-filtered HDR levels, a 1.35 luminance threshold with a 0.5 soft knee, and strength 0.06. Extraction includes only scoped corridor radiance. Final composition adds that linear energy before exposure/tone/gamma; other-sector pixels use the legacy bypass. Feature-off renders (`shadows=0`, `probe=0`, `fog=0`, `bloom=0`) expose actual contributions of the same candidate. The shadow switch disables surface/volume receiver lookups after the room probe is baked; the cached reflection probe is held constant, including its captured occlusion. This isolates live shadow contribution rather than rebuilding an entirely unshadowed environment.

`getHdrDiagnostics()` copies the actual scene and composite GPU textures on demand, decodes half floats and reports HDR peaks, samples above one, finite/negative values and coverage. It is not called in the normal frame loop. The standalone calibration probe also verifies real RGBA16F + 4× MSAA, known HDR values, one display transform and an exact display bypass. These are correctness checks, not artistic or hardware-performance acceptance.

All implementation is original native WebGPU/WGSL. No code or assets were imported from Abyssal Garden; its optional architectural example did not add a runtime dependency. No reference image enters the build. Observatory/hangar source assets, original particle RNG stream, camera poses and flight points remain covered by regression tests.
